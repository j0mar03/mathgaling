cover letter or synopsis as required initial uploads. Keep identifying sheets out
of the anonymous review PDF. Do not send unsolicited documents to the committee.
This guide and draft letters do not send messages or submit anything.

SUPPORTING-SOURCE ZIP
build/author-support-source.zip is a separate working package. It includes the four
standalone supporting .tex documents, shared submission_details.tex and generated
number files, plus the completion/metadata/guidance texts. It excludes the SLR,
raw CSVs, credentials and analysis inputs. It is not an anonymous review-paper ZIP.

OVERLEAF USE
Open the supporting ZIP as a separate project, or add its files to your existing
project. Select titlepage.tex, declarations.tex, coverletter.tex or synopsis.tex
as the main document to typeset that supporting file. Keep all included inputs in
the project root. Restore main.tex as the main document for the actual paper.
All four use article format as administrative documents. Do not replace the IEEE
class or layout of the conference manuscript with the SLR's Springer formatting.

FINALIZATION ORDER
1. Complete AUTHOR_COMPLETION_FORM.txt and submission_details.tex from actual facts.
2. Reconcile those facts with main.tex's ethics/setting/cohort/overlap paragraphs.
3. Review all results and citations; preserve the approved method and actual results.
4. Export and upload both diagrams; compile and inspect the anonymous review PDF.
5. Resolve author notes before hiding them. Remove supporting-draft banners and
   pending-letter prompts only after each document is truthful and ready to use.
6. Enter verified author metadata in the portal, upload the required review file,
   check the submission preview and retain the confirmation receipt/identifier.
7. After acceptance, follow instructions for identified camera-ready files,
   publication forms, registration and presentation. Do not assume those files or
   payments are needed for initial review unless the organizer says so.

Source files still contain pending facts. Creating these files does not resolve
missing approval references, authorship, setting or a final compiled-PDF check.
