\textbf{Subject: Full-paper submission for presentation and proceedings publication}

Dear Program Committee,

Please consider the manuscript entitled ``\AAIEPaperTitle'' for AAIE2027.
The paper evaluates whether the contextual fuzzy adjustments implemented in an
elementary mathematics tutor improve subsequent-answer predictions compared with
the same Bayesian knowledge tracing update without those adjustments.

The source-pinned offline comparison uses \ScoredTargets{} common prediction
targets from 92 account identifiers, identical fixed BKT parameters and
prediction-before-update ordering. Standard BKT achieved a Brier score of
\BktBrier{}, compared with \HybridBrier{} for the adjusted model. The paired
difference was \BrierDelta{}, with an account-bootstrap 95\% interval of
[\BrierCILower{}, \BrierCIUpper{}]. The paper reports no clear predictive benefit
under this configuration and explains the outcome, timing, cohort and deployment
limitations. It does not claim improved learning outcomes.

The contribution is a reproducible evaluation of an implemented contextual
adjustment, aligned with the conference's topics of intelligent tutoring systems,
adaptive learning and educational data mining.

The related earlier paper, ``Adoption of Bayesian Knowledge Tracing With Fuzzy
Logic in the Development of Personalized Math Learning System for Grade 3 and 4,''
is cited in the manuscript. It reports system development and teacher evaluation;
the current manuscript reports a predictive comparison. Its DOI is
\url{https://doi.org/10.22492/issn.2186-5892.2026.131}.
