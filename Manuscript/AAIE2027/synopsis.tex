\section*{Problem and research question}
Adding contextual rules to a learner model does not by itself establish improved
prediction. This study asks whether the implemented fuzzy adjustments improve
subsequent application-graded answer predictions compared with standard BKT.
\section*{Study design}
The approved retrospective replay retains \CandidateResponseCount{} sequential
nonpractice responses from \CandidateAccountCount{} Grade 3--4 account identifiers
during July 2--4, 2025. Both models use the same fixed BKT parameters, initial
states and observed trajectories. The first response in each of 394 student--KC
sequences updates state only, leaving \ScoredTargets{} common subsequent targets.
Each probability is generated before the target response can update its state.
\section*{Measured findings}
Standard BKT has a Brier score of \BktBrier{}, compared with \HybridBrier{} for
the adjusted model. The hybrid-minus-BKT difference is \BrierDelta{}, with an
account-cluster bootstrap 95\% interval of [\BrierCILower{}, \BrierCIUpper{}].
Log loss is \BktLogLoss{} and \HybridLogLoss{}, respectively. Pooled AUROC is
\BktAUC{} and \HybridAUC{}, respectively. The interval spans zero; the evaluated
adjustment therefore shows no clear predictive benefit under this configuration.
The findings do not establish equivalence or universal failure of fuzzy modeling.
\section*{Contribution and conference fit}
The paper isolates the incremental effect of an existing contextual update using
source-pinned execution, common targets, paired scoring and uncertainty estimates.
It aligns with intelligent tutoring systems, adaptive learning and educational
data mining. The contribution is a predictive evaluation, not an estimate of
learning gains or the effects of an alternative task-selection policy.
\section*{Relationship to prior work}
The cited Math Tagumpay IAFOR paper reports system development and teacher
evaluation. This follow-up evaluates student-linked answer predictions; the
teacher ratings are not reused as new findings. The separate SystematicITS review
supplies the organizational example for these supporting files only, not evidence
or results for the AAIE study.
\section*{Limits and pending author information}
Outcomes are reconstructed using the historical application validator; timing is
cumulative; account eligibility remains unverified; and the replayed Express
algorithm is not established as the historical online deployment. No fitted-model
benchmark or component ablation is claimed. Ethics approval is reported, while
the actual reference, consent details, setting, author list and final declarations
remain to be completed from study-specific records.
